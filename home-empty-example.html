    About Pagan Events

    We list Pagan, New Age and broadly similar events, fayres, markets and moots taking place across the United Kingdom. Browsing is free and always will be, and there is nothing to sign up for.

    Sun and moon rise and set times are worked out from the postcode you give, and the Wheel of the Year shows the eight festivals with their dates.

    Event Organisers

    We often receive emails asking why some events are not listed. The simple answer is that we can only list events we know about. This site gets several hundred visitors every day from Pagans looking for events such as yours.

    To maximise publicity for your event, please either let us know about it or add it yourself — free of charge.
